### 2. Disputes & Complaints (NEW)
**Location:** After "Price Negotiation & Booking" section

**5 Detailed FAQ Items:**

1. **What can I do if there's a problem with a service?**
   - Two-phase approach: Direct resolution first, then formal complaint
   - Direct resolution steps (contact provider, explain, give time)
   - Formal complaint process (file through dashboard)

2. **What's the deadline for filing a complaint?**
   - 14-day deadline from service completion
   - Why timing matters
   - Tip about filing immediately

3. **What happens when I file a complaint?**
   - 6-step investigation process
   - Acknowledgment → Review → Provider Response → Investigation → Decision → Notification
   - Typical 5-10 business day timeline

4. **What are the possible outcomes of a complaint?**
   - 5 possible outcomes: Dismiss, Warning, Suspension, Permanent Ban, Legal Recommendation
   - Note about platform's facilitator role
   - Guidance on pursuing legal remedies for major disputes

5. **Can I rate and review if there was a problem?**
   - Yes, absolute right to honest reviews after problems
   - How reviews help others
   - Guidelines for fair, honest reviews (specific, constructive, non-defamatory)
   - Importance for platform trust
